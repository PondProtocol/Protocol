## Before you open Trade

Complete every step in the checklist — Begin, both assets, Verify Issuer,
Connect Wallet, Set Trust Lines, then this page. There is **no buy button**
here. Buying starts only after issuance, in a market this site has verified.

## Official terminal

[Open Trade](/trade/) — WalletConnect or Xaman only. Never a seed.

What Trade is today:

- A place to connect a wallet you control
- A preview surface, not a live $PND book or AMM
- Not a claim, not a FirstLedger shortcut, not a guessed DEX path

What Trade is not:

- A live $PND market
- A place this site invents TVL, volume, or issued supply
- A reason to skip [Verify Issuer](/verify/)

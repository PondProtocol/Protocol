## What will be verified first

Before any trade flow is enabled, this page will show:

- the exact $PND identity: currency code `PND` plus the canonical issuer
  address;
- the XRPL market or AMM pool used for the transaction;
- the quoted amount, price impact, and slippage before signing;
- the destination wallet and the exact XRPL transaction being requested.

The site will never ask for a seed phrase, private key, wallet import, or
claim payment.

## Wallet support

When a live market exists, the intended signing options are self-custodial
wallets such as Xaman and WalletConnect-compatible XRPL wallets, including
Joey Wallet. A wallet connection only signs a transaction; it does not create
liquidity or guarantee a price.

For now, start with [Verify the issuer](/verify/) and read [how to hold
safely](/hold/). The target $PND launch date is **1 October 2026**; $rPND is
a separate asset and is not part of that launch.

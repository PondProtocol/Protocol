# $rPND

$rPND is the planned Multi-Purpose Token (MPT) companion to $PND.

**$rPND has not been created.** Its `MPTokenIssuanceID`, circulating supply,
and final economics do not exist on ledger yet. This section explains the
design and identity of the planned asset; it is not a launch or trading page.

<div class="callout callout-critical">
<p><strong>Not the 1 October 2026 launch.</strong> That date is for $PND.
$rPND requires a separate MPT creation step and should not be presented as
already issued.</p>
</div>

## What links it to $PND

The planned metadata may identify paired currency code `PND` for indexers.
That is documentation, not an on-ledger peg, redemption right, or conversion
path. The two assets remain separate ledger objects.

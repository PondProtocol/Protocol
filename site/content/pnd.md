# $PND

The Pond Protocol issued currency on the XRP Ledger.

**$PND has not launched yet.** The target is **1 October 2026**. No $PND has
been issued, and a token trading under the three-character code `PND` is not
automatically this asset.

## Start with identity

asset specific. Verify it before you trust a balance, listing, or link.

## Supply and launch

The working policy supply is **100 billion $PND**. That is issuer policy, not a
ledger-enforced cap. The public split and launch status are documented
separately on the [supply split](/vesting/) page.

$rPND is a different asset and is **not** launching on 1 October 2026. Read
the [two-token comparison](/pnd-and-rpnd/) before treating the two names as
interchangeable.
